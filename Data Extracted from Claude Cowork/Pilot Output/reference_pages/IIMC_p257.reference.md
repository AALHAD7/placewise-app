# PRIMETIME BID PLAY

Your client is a media & entertainment giant & is looking to bid for a global event. You are required to determine how much should the client bid.

## KEY LEARNINGS
- The ambiguity around "margin" was a critical point. Ensure you have a precise definition of financial objectives before starting calculations.
- In such cases, it is most important that you first lay down a structure in your mind as to how you will calculate the final required parameter
- A complete analysis must always acknowledge and test the key assumptions.

## APPROACH
- Bid Price
  - Profit
    - Total Revenue
      - Primetime Revenue
        - Total primetime hours -> seconds
        - Primetime rates / second
      - Non Primetime Revenue
        - Total non – primetime hours -> seconds
        - Non – primetime rates / second
    - Total Costs
  - 1.3 (30% margin requirement)

## BROWNIE POINTS
- Go beyond just advertising. Briefly consider other potential revenue streams like licensing fees, syndication of highlights, or subscription revenue for a more comprehensive valuation.
- Instead of accepting a single cost number, break it down into key components like the bid price itself, production costs and marketing costs to calculate a more realistic maximum bid.
